About balco.nyc

An independent, free calculator for plug-in balcony solar, built from public data and open-source code.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

What balco.nyc is

balco.nyc is an independent, free balcony-solar calculator: it estimates what a plug-in solar panel would produce and save at any US address, with a 3D shade model for New York City buildings. It runs NREL’s PVWatts simulation for the exact address, values the output at the local price of electricity, and reports the result as a planning estimate with its assumptions shown. In New York City it also builds a 3D model of the surrounding block from city building data, so the shade cast by the buildings opposite is measured rather than guessed.

It is free, carries no advertising and no affiliate links, and does not sell, install, rank or recommend any product. The code is open source on GitHub.

What it is not

  • Not a vendor or installer, and not paid by one. Not affiliated with the City of New York, Con Edison, NYSERDA, NREL, the EIA, the EPA, or any seller of solar equipment.
  • Not legal advice. The rules for plug-in solar differ by state and by building, and the site says when a state has not been reviewed.
  • Not a guarantee. NYC estimates carry a modeled uncertainty range of about 15%; estimates elsewhere rest on a description of the view and a state-average price, so their range is wider.

How the numbers are made

Every figure on the site comes from a public source that is named where it is used: NREL PVWatts for solar output, the U.S. Energy Information Administration for state electricity prices, EPA eGRID for grid emissions, NYC PLUTO and building footprints for New York buildings, Con Edison’s tariff for the New York rate, and state legislatures’ own bill pages for plug-in solar law. The methodology documents every formula and assumption, and the test suite in the repository checks that the numbers stated on the site agree with the code.

Each guide shows the date its claims were last checked, and the site fails its own build when a page goes too long without a review.

Citing balco.nyc

Please attribute as “balco.nyc, an independent balcony solar calculator for US addresses, with a 3D shade model for New York City” with a link to https://balco.nyc/. A machine-readable summary for AI assistants is at /llms.txt.

Corrections

If a figure is wrong or out of date, please open an issue on GitHub. Corrections are made in the source and noted in the page’s review date.
