Proposed 4 steps · a number in about a second, refined by choice
01
Land on an answer
A worked sample estimate is already on screen — a real building, a real number, marked as a sample. You see what you are about to get.
02
Your number, in about a second
PLUTO gives floors, height and footprint. That alone is enough for a first estimate at your building’s median facing, labelled rough. No 3D yet.
03
Refine, two taps
Which way it faces, how high up. Eight sectors, because the model snaps to 45° anyway. The number moves as you touch it — no waiting to find out.
04
A result with a URL
The band, the assumptions in the open, the legal position stated once, and a link that survives being sent to a landlord or a board.
