The SUNNY Act · S8512 / A9111

Passed. Passed. Awaiting one signature.

Balcony solar is legal in Germany, and illegal in New York, for one reason: the state still requires utility approval for any device that pushes power into a household circuit. One bill removes that. Here is where it stands, and what it would be worth on your balcony.
